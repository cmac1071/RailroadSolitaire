How the Tracks Collapse

Railroad is a game of alignment and chain reactions. 52 cards are dealt one-by-one into a single row. Your success depends on the relationship between the 1st and 4th cards from the right.

The Rank Match

If the 1st and 4th cards share the same Rank (e.g., two Kings), all four cards in that set are discarded.

The Cascade: Every time cards are removed, the row collapses. This can trigger a chain reaction, allowing you to clear massive sections of the track in a single move.

The ultimate goal? Clear all 52 cards for a score of zero.
