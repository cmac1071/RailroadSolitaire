# Railroad! Solitaire Support

## How to Play

Deal cards one at a time by tapping the deck. When the newest card matches the card three positions back:

- **Suit match**: The two middle cards are discarded
- **Rank match**: All four cards are discarded

Clear all 52 cards to win! The odds are approximately 1 in 142.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I start a new game?**
A: Tap the "New" button at the bottom of the screen.

**Q: Where are my statistics stored?**
A: All statistics are stored locally on your device. They are not synced to the cloud or shared with anyone.

**Q: Can I reset my statistics?**
A: Currently, statistics cannot be reset. This feature may be added in a future update.

**Q: Why do my statistics show games I didn't finish?**
A: A game is counted when you start it. If you tap "New" before finishing, the incomplete game is recorded with your score at that point.

**Q: Is there sound?**
A: Yes! Make sure your device is not in silent mode. You can adjust sound volume or disable sounds in the Menu.
